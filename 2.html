Objective
Score as many runs as possible against the AI opponent using hand gestures (1–6). If your number matches the AI's number, you are OUT.
✋
Hand Gestures
Show 1–5 fingers for runs, or a thumbs up 👍 for 6.
Each gesture = one ball.
1 → 1 run
2 → 2 runs
3 → 3 runs
4 → 4 runs
5 → 5 runs
👍 → 6
🤖
AI Strategy
The AI gets smarter as you play:
Balls 1–5 Random guesses
Balls 6–12 Learns your top number
Ball 13+ Danger Zone — predicts recent moves
🏏
Scoring
Your num ≠ AI num → Runs = your number added to score

Your num = AI num → OUT — innings over!

Strike Rate is tracked in real time.
SR = (Runs / Balls) × 100
🏆
Winner Selection
Winners are ranked in this order:

1st Highest total runs
2nd Higher Strike Rate (if runs tied)
3rd Fewer balls played (if still tied)
📷
Camera Mode
The game uses MediaPipe hand tracking to detect your fingers live.

Tip Good lighting works best.
Tip Keep hand inside the frame.

If camera fails, use the manual buttons 1–6 below the camera.
